OUR STORY

Rooted in Craft.
Refined by Passion.

Nutoria was founded on a singular conviction: that the natural dry fruits bestowed upon humanity by ancient soils are culinary gems of the highest order, requiring zero artificial intervention and total respect for harvest cycles.

From our origins in Kashmir’s high-altitude walnut groves to our deep grower partnerships spanning Rafsanjan, Malatya, and the Jordan Valley, every harvest represents twenty-five years of unrelenting devotion to perfection.

The Odyssey

A Quarter Century of Dedication

Key moments that built India's most respected dry-fruits atelier.

1998

The First Himalayan Harvest

Our founders began by personally hand-selecting wild kagzi walnuts in Shopian, Kashmir, introducing cold nitrogen sealed batches to select families in Mumbai.

2005

The Nutoria Atelier is Born

Formalizing the brand as Nutoria, establishing our signature caliber grading system where only nuts in the top 3rd percentile are certified for royal packaging.

2015

D2C Farm-to-Connoisseur Launch

Eliminating distributors and intermediaries entirely to deliver fresh harvest directly within 48 hours of optical sorting and vacuum nitrogen sealing.

2020

10,000 Private Collectors

Crossed 10,000 active members in our private allocation circle, expanding our direct sourcing network into the sacred Ajwa palms of Al Madinah.

2024

Launch of Imperial Versailles Reserve

Unveiled our ultra-luxury bespoke gifting trunks, pairing Cartier-inspired teakwood & gold finishes with colossal King W180 cashews and Iranian Mamra almonds.

Our Philosophy

Values Cast in Stone

Guiding principles that steer every single procurement decision we make.

01

Authenticity

Every nut can be traced back to its specific valley of origin. We never blend harvests or mask origins with generic labels.

02

Zero Compromise Purity

No sulphur dioxide, no chemical preservatives, no paraffin waxes. Just 100% pure sunshine, soil nutrients, and clean nitrogen seal.

03

Sustainable Soils

We work exclusively with multigenerational farms that practice natural soil replenishment, clean water conservation, and regenerative farming.

04

Michelin-Grade Craft

From precision hand-calibrated sizing to artisanal cold-smoke batches, our treatments honor the ingredient like fine haute gastronomy.
