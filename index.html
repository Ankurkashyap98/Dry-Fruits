The Nutoria Standard

The Four Tenets of Excellence

Why connoisseurs, royal households, and master pastry chefs trust our harvest.

01

100% Nitrogen Flush

Every tin and canister is evacuated of oxygen and sealed with food-grade pure nitrogen within hours of cracking. No rancidity, no oxidation.

02

Royal Caliber Grading

Only the top 3% percentile of kernel sizes pass our double optical laser and manual sorting tables. Every nut is colossal and uniform.

03

Direct Orchard Fair Trade

No middle brokers. We partner directly with generational farmers across Kashmir, Iran, and the Aegean to ensure authentic heritage roots.

04

Zero Chemical Bleach

Natural color reflects real nutrient density. We never use chlorine, sulphur dioxide, or synthetic glazes on our dried fruits.

The Heritage

Rooted in Craft.
Refined by Passion.

Nutoria began as an intimate family passion in the high valleys of Kashmir, hand-selecting walnuts that possessed sweet, unbitter oils. Over a quarter century, our pursuit expanded globally—seeking out rare Persian Mamra trees that drink only snowmelt, and ancient Medjool groves in Jericho.

We treat dry fruits not as commodities, but as precious jewels of the earth, deserving the same reverence as grand cru wines.
